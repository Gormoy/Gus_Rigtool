Onion skin (your first script, made to work on Maya 2024 without PyMEL) - ghosts drawn IN FRONT of the
character, like Toon Boom Harmony. Based on onionSkinRenderer by Viele (MIT licence, see LICENSE).

INSTALL OR UPDATE (every new version the same way)
1. Unzip anywhere (for example Downloads).
2. Drag drag_to_maya.mel into Maya's viewport. It installs (or updates) the onion skin, puts one "Onion" button
   on the shelf you have open and opens the window. From then on: click Onion. Your settings are kept.

USE
1. Select a control of each character (or their meshes, or a group) and press + in Target Objects.
   A control adds its whole character's meshes. Two characters: one control of each.
2. Click once in the viewport of your camera, then Toggle Renderer.
3. Past / next keys (on by default, 1 past + 1 next): each character's own keys - nothing needs to stay
   selected. Turn more on in the list (the eye icons) if you want.
4. Fill onion skins: shows them straight away. After changing keys, press Fill again.
5. Draw behind is OFF: ghosts in front of the character. Tick it to put them behind.
Colours: past blue, next orange (change them in the window).

EXTRAS (the box under Toggle Renderer)
- Onion Skin Type, with Harmony's names: Original colours (shaded) / Coloured (flat) / Coloured outline.
- Fade further away: each ghost further from now gets lighter (100 = no fading).
- Main keys only: key ghosts only on your key poses, skipping breakdowns (keys set with Key > Breakdown) -
  check your extremes like a 2D key pass.
- Auto fill: stop on a frame and the missing ghosts fill by themselves (never during playback).
- Playblast with onion skins: a pencil test of the playback range with the ghosts in it.

ARCS (the Arcs box) - track arcs like on paper
1. Select vertices (nose tip, ear tips, a hand) or controls, then press + Add selected points (up to 12).
2. Stop on a frame: a line shows where each point is, 24 frames back (past colour) and 24 forward (next
   colour), with a dot on each frame - dots close together = slow, far apart = fast. Keys get a bigger dot,
   your frame a white one.
3. Frames before / Frames after: how far it reaches. A dot every: 1 = every frame, 2 = every 2nd...
4. Change a key: the line is recorded again by itself a moment later. Nothing is added to your scene; the
   points are forgotten when you open another scene. Playblast with onion skins shows them too.

HOTKEYS (put them on keys you like)
Windows > Settings/Preferences > Hotkey Editor > Custom Scripts: OnionSkin_Toggle, OnionSkin_Fill,
OnionSkin_NextKey, OnionSkin_PreviousKey, OnionSkin_ToggleArcs (next / previous key of the onion characters - flip through your keys
like paper). Pick one, then type the key you want in the Hotkey Editor.

What changed from the original script:
- no PyMEL needed (onionSkinRenderer/mayacompat.py);
- + adds a control's whole character; key ghosts use the characters' own keys (onionSkinRenderer/extras.py);
- in front by default; Toggle works in the viewport you last clicked; old pictures cleared when the camera
  changes, the viewport is resized or you toggle; Fill button; a warning when the camera moves;
- drag_to_maya.mel installer with a shelf button.
