Onion skin (your first script, made to work on Maya 2024 without PyMEL) - ghosts drawn IN FRONT of the
character, like Toon Boom Harmony, plus arcs. Based on onionSkinRenderer by Viele (MIT licence, see LICENSE).

INSTALL OR UPDATE (every new version the same way)
1. Unzip anywhere (for example Downloads).
2. Drag drag_to_maya.mel into Maya's viewport. It installs (or updates) the onion skin, puts one "Onion" button
   on the shelf you have open and opens the window. From then on: click Onion. Your settings are kept.

START
1. Click once in your camera view, then the big button at the top: "Onion skin ON in this view" (green).
   Click it again to turn it off. Closing the window does NOT turn the onion skin off.
2. Ghosts tab > Characters: select any control of a character, press + Add selected character.
   Two characters: one control of each. The eye hides that character's ghosts (it stays in the list).
3. The line under the ON button says what is happening: how many ghosts show, how many are waiting.

GHOSTS TAB
- All ghosts: Opacity (all ghosts lighter or stronger at once) and Hide ghosts while playing (on: playback as
  fast as without; the ghosts come back when you stop).
- Each character's ghosts sit on ITS OWN keys (the Hare's on the Hare's keys, the Fox's on the Fox's).
- Ghosts on: Keys (the characters' own keys) / Main keys (breakdowns skipped - keys set with Key > Breakdown)
  / Frames (every N frames).
- Before / After: how many ghosts each side (- / + buttons, up to 12). Every: for Frames (4 = a ghost every
  4 frames).
- Fade further: each ghost further away is lighter (100 = no fading).
- Look: Shaded / Flat colour / Outline (Harmony's three looks).
- Light table: "The others see-through" - the characters you're not animating become see-through (their own
  colours), the one you animate stays solid. Which one you animate follows your selection: pick a Hare control
  and the Fox fades. See-through: how much. Ghosts of: All characters / Only the one I animate (the others'
  ghosts very faint). Needs two characters or more; it costs one extra drawing of the scene while it's on.
- Colours: Blue / Orange, Red / Green (Harmony's), or Mine... (pick past, then next).
- Advanced: one bar per ghost - drag it up or down for that ghost's opacity; all the way down = that ghost off.

PRESETS (the buttons above the tabs)
- Blocking (keys): 2 key ghosts each side.  Spacing: a ghost every frame, 3 each side.
- Arcs +-24: a ghost every 4 frames, 6 each side (24 frames), and the arcs on.
- + Save mine keeps what you have now under a name. Right-click one of yours to delete it.

ARCS TAB - track arcs like on paper
1. Select vertices (nose tip, ear tips, a hand) or controls, then + Add selected points (up to 12).
2. Stop on a frame: a line shows where each point is, 24 frames back (past colour) and 24 forward (next
   colour), with dots - close together = slow, far apart = fast. Keys get a bigger dot, your frame a white one.
3. Before / After: how far it reaches. A dot every 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 frames. Each point has its own eye and x.
4. Change a key: the line is recorded again by itself a moment later. Nothing is added to your scene; points
   are forgotten when you open another scene.

SETTINGS TAB
- Auto fill: stop on a frame and the missing ghosts fill by themselves (never during playback).
- Draw behind the character (off = in front, like Harmony).
- Pinned frames: + This frame keeps that frame's ghost wherever you go.
- Pictures kept / Forget pictures, Outline width, Playblast with onion skins (a pencil test with the ghosts and
  arcs), Show the mini bar.
Fill (top): takes all the pictures now. With many frames a progress bar shows - Esc stops it.

MINI BAR
A small bar over Maya: ON, Before - / +, After - / +, Keys / Main / Frames, Opacity, Hide (while playing), Fill,
Arcs, Light (light table), and the gear (opens the window). Drag it by its left edge; it remembers where it was and comes back next time. x closes it.

HOTKEYS (put them on keys you like)
Windows > Settings/Preferences > Hotkey Editor > Custom Scripts: OnionSkin_Toggle, OnionSkin_Fill,
OnionSkin_NextKey, OnionSkin_PreviousKey (flip through your keys like paper), OnionSkin_ToggleArcs,
OnionSkin_MiniBar. Pick one, then type the key you want in the Hotkey Editor.

What changed from the original script:
- no PyMEL needed (onionSkinRenderer/mayacompat.py);
- a control adds its whole character; key ghosts use the characters' own keys (extras.py);
- in front by default; old pictures cleared when the camera changes, the viewport is resized or you toggle;
- arcs (arcs.py); a new window and mini bar (window.py, minibar.py, widgets.py) over one shared state
  (session.py); hide ghosts while playing, a picture per character (its own keys), the light table and the
  see-through look 3 in the shaders (core.py, onionSkinShader.*);
- drag_to_maya.mel installer with a shelf button and hotkey commands.
