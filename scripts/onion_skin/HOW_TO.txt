Onion Skin 1.0 - see-through 3D ghosts of the past / next keys, in every camera, for several characters.
Maya 2024+ (Windows or Mac). No PyMEL or other tools needed (standalone).

INSTALL OR UPDATE (every new version the same way)
1. Unzip anywhere (for example Downloads).
2. Drag drag_to_maya.mel from the OnionSkin folder into Maya's viewport.
   It installs Onion Skin (or replaces the old version), puts one "Onion" button on the shelf you have open,
   and opens the window. From then on: click Onion. Your settings are kept.

USE
1. Select something of each character - a control is enough (it stands for the whole character), or meshes,
   or a group - and press Add selected. Two characters? Add one control of each.
2. Show: Past / next keys (each character's own keys - nothing needs to stay selected) or Every N frames.
   Past / Next: how many ghosts each side (0-5).
3. Live is on: stop on a frame and the ghosts update (not during playback - they catch up when it stops).
   Update does it by hand; Clear ghosts removes them (the list stays).
4. Colours, Opacity, Fade further away: past is blue, next orange by default.
5. Hide (for playblasts) hides them in one click. They're never saved into your file, and closing the window
   removes everything the tool made.

THE TEST IN REAL MAYA (optional, a minute)
Double-click run_test.bat (Windows) or run_test.command (Mac: in Terminal, bash run_test.command).
It builds two small keyed characters in an empty scene - your files aren't touched - and writes
tests\onion_test_results.txt. Send that file if anything says FAIL.
