Gus Animtool, the new look (mock-up · nothing built)

The same principles as the Rigtool: a sidebar in the order an animator works (Pick, Pose, Keys, Spaces, Limbs; Settings its own button), a colour per area, the character and frame range always on top, cards with pictures that show what a tool does, the next step, nothing cut off. Every tool of today stays (57); none are lost.
