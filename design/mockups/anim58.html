4. The pie: hold ` (backtick) over the viewport

Hold the key, move toward a tool, let go = it runs. Each tool's NAME UNDER its icon. Top: the profiles (press 1-8). Bottom: the rest of the profile (more than 8 tools). Right-click a tool: the pie stays open with its options. Needs a Maya fact first (does the hold-and-release key work in your Maya), then a try-it script and your screenshot.
