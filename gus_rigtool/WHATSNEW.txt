RIGGING
- Pivots: the bottom / centre / any spot of a box, from a face or vertices, along a chain; remember a pivot and
  mirror it to the other side.
- Groups: offset / sdk groups above or around anything, where you want them; remove groups; saved stacks.
- Select: by type, inside groups, twins, skin joints, the joints a control drives, a ribbon's controls; quick
  sets and recent selections.
- Mirror: controls and poses, plus the links between them (follows, spaces, attributes, constraints, pivots).
  Mirror a setup: constraints and utility nodes you built by hand on one side, with the groups they need.
- Joint at the centre of a selection (per object or per piece), and Make controls for your own joints
  (FK chains, constrain / parent / direct, both sides at once).
- Ribbons for anything, not only the face: rebuild with more or fewer controls (the skin is kept), replace the
  surface, switch how the joints follow (constraints / copying), mirror the sides, make a loop exactly
  symmetric; a surface lofted the wrong way is turned for you.
- Face kit: eyes, eyelids with Blink, mouth (lips follow the jaw by height), brows and strips from the mesh's
  edges; Redo a part later from the edges it remembers.
- Follow: pick its pivot and axes, move the pivot, update, copy, mirror to the twin.
- Skin: a weight list (exact values on vertices, average, lock); mirror checks for missing twins first.
- Cloth (Skin page): garments skinned like the body + Delta Mush, ribbons on their edges, a cloth layer
  (ngSkinTools when loaded), a range of motion to test them, a fix for the pose where they break; Repair
  weights for a garment pulled to the world origin.
- Controls in front of the mesh (Always Draw On Top): on for new rigs' body controls, a button for every control.
- Chain jiggle: reacts to whatever moves the chain (any direction, the world control too), with a limit towards
  the body; undo stays clean.
- Stop at the body: rotation limits on a chain's controls measured against the body mesh (a cape can't go in).
- Limits: how far controls can move / turn, from the pose too, mirrored to the other side (Attributes page).
- Bendy arms and legs: a bend control on the knee / elbow (both pieces bend into it) with a 'roundness' slider.
- Publish and Deliver work on a rig made entirely by hand too (no Build needed): its top group and controls are
  found, the groups above the controls are locked, joints hidden, sets made - Unpublish puts it all back.

NODES
- Nodes page (Connect): name utility nodes from their connections (multiplyDivide13 ->
  L_Brow_02_ty_to_fol_11_sdk_md), select a whole setup, trace a channel back to its control, compare a
  setup with its twin, find offsets left at rest and loops; Make a driver (a channel's positive / negative
  side x an amount drives other channels, both sides at once); Connect by name pattern (L_Brow_0*_ctrl).

CHECKING
- Check this scene: turns the world control and lists what doesn't come along (double transforms, nodes left
  behind), then puts it back.
- Check this scene: a read-only diagnosis of your character (ribbons, follows, labels, skin, stuck controls,
  fixes, face kit, guides, connections, picker), problems first.
- Repair: what the checks found that can be fixed safely, ticked and fixed (each its own undo step).
- Accept warnings: mark a warning as intended; it passes until something new shows up on that line.
- Maya check and Self-test (Settings): every tool and the rig builder tested in your Maya, with a report.
- Speed report (Settings): how long the tools take on your character, slowest first.

ANIMATORS (Gus Animtool, in every delivery)
- Works on any rig, hand-made ones too: it finds the controls and the left / right names by itself.
- Picker: snapping with guide lines, resize several buttons, space evenly, same size; new controls are offered
  on their own page.
- Your picker travels inside the rig (Save in the rig's own file); animators can keep their own, export /
  import it, or go back to the rig's.
- Pose library: folders, search, rename, update pose / picture, preview (mirrored too) with Keep / Back.
- Referenced rigs added after the window opened just work (no more 'Value is out of range').
- Mirror finds a control's twin by position when the names don't pair (a mouth loop numbered around).

FASTER
- The main window opens in about a second (pages are built when you first open them).
- Hiding / isolating body parts and finding the controls are several times faster.
